01 THE PROBLEM

Repairs get lost in WhatsApp threads and forwarded emails.

A tenant reports a leak. The landlord forwards it to a contractor. The contractor sends a quote by SMS. The invoice arrives weeks later in a different channel. FixFlow replaces that chaos with one timeline everyone can see.

Landlords & managers
