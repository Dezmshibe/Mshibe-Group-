Privacy Policy

How Mshibe Group handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the feature you use, the website may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, telephone number, delivery address, appointment details, order details, and messages submitted through contact forms.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to provide and demonstrate requested services, process food orders or appointment requests, respond to enquiries, maintain the website, improve our products, and protect the website from misuse.

3. Demo experiences

The Food and Appointments areas may operate as demonstrations of Mshibe Group products. Information entered into a demo may be stored by the application for demonstration and testing purposes. Please do not submit unnecessary sensitive or confidential information.

4. Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers or technology providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website or provide a requested service, or where required by law.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and session data

The website may use essential session data and similar technologies to keep features such as shopping carts, logins, and website functionality working. We may introduce additional analytics or functionality as the website develops.

7. Your choices

You can contact us to ask questions about information submitted through the website or to request assistance with your information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact MshibeGroup@gmail.com.

This policy is a general website privacy policy and may be expanded as Mshibe Group adds production services, analytics, payment providers, or other third-party integrations.
